About Me

I am Blessings Chuulu, a highly skilled and certified technology professional with a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from the University of Zambia, specializing in Computer Networks Engineering. My expertise is validated by industry-recognized certifications from Cisco, AWS, and Google Cloud, forming a robust foundation in both on-premise and cloud infrastructure. Hardworking, determined and self motivated. Love listening to music and reading.

My theoretical knowledge was solidified during a hands-on internship at Zambia Railways Limited, where I managed critical IT infrastructure—gaining practical experience in network installation, Wi-Fi configuration, hardware repair, and software troubleshooting. This immersive journey provided a real-world context for my certified skills in networking and cloud platforms.

Beyond networking, I am a versatile full-stack developer and a creative graphic designer, enabling me to not only build and maintain robust systems but also create intuitive user interfaces and engaging visual experiences. This unique combination of skills allows me to approach projects with a holistic perspective, from the backend architecture and network security to the frontend design and user interaction.

Guided by strong personal values and a leadership mindset honed as a first-born, I am passionate about leveraging this diverse technical toolkit to solve complex problems and deliver integrated, impactful solutions.
